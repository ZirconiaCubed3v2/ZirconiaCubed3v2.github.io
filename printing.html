3D Printing

I've decided I need money, so now I print stuff for people. Here's all the stuff I have, and my printer(s), updated after each job so you don't need to ask what I have.

Want something made? Email me with an STL or a 3MF and tell me any other specifics, and I'll give you a quote. Yes you have to pay, this isn't a free service.

Pricing may change depending on how many requests I get, but currently here it is:

  • $10 base fee
  • $0.05 per gram for low-temp (PLA); $0.10 per gram for exotic (PETG, TPU); $0.15 per gram for high-temp (ABS, ASA)
  • $10 per discreet hour (under 1 hour is $0, 1-2 hours is $10, 2-3 hours is $20, etc)
  • No charge for reprints that are my problem (e.g. if the print fails because I was an idiot or some other non-model-related issue)
  • I DO NOT DO POST PROCESSING OTHER THAN SUPPORT REMOVAL OR VERY SLIGHT DEBURRING
  • Basically, I'll make sure you get a nice print, but don't expect any sanding, smoothing, painting, or anything like that. Trust me, you wouldn't want that from me.
